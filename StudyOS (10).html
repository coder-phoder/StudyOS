How to use Meridian

Type or speak naturally. Examples:

  • brief me — full snapshot of your day
  • what should I do next? — priority suggestions
  • what's left — unfinished tasks & topics
  • remind me to finish physics notes — add a to-do
  • log 30 minutes of Python — any language works (C, Java, C++…)
  • my goals / my grades / study plan
  • plan of the week — full 7-day study plan
  • what's today / schedule Lab tomorrow at 2pm
  • help — full command list anytime

Offline · typos are OK · say help if you forget
